A messenger application that doesn't sell your data.

Built by gamers for gamers.

Venta.gg is a new but powerful platform for instant messaging and WebRTC. Built for communities, groups, and friends.

What works today

Instant Messaging

Send messages instantly to your friends and communities.

WebRTC Calls

Real-time video and audio calls with up to 4 participants.

Guilds

Community spaces with channels, categories, wiki pages, and a granular permission system.

Friend Relationships

Add friends and manage your social graph with private conversations and calls.

End-to-End Encryption

All private messages and calls are encrypted via MLS - no one but you and your friends can read them.
